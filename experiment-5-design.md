# Experiment 5 — loss-matched capability profiles on Pythia's public checkpoints: Prediction 3 at lab scale

**Status: DESIGN (session 1 of three), written 2026-09-22 on Michael's word ("Design Exp 5"). §10's dials await his ruling. No model has been contacted; the Hub was read once for metadata (§2). The design session ran four analysis-only computations on committed bytes, all listed in §2; two of them changed what the primary statistic is, and §2 says how.**

## 1. The question

The essay's Prediction 3: *models trained to the same pre-training loss on the same distribution should show the same capability profile even at very different sizes, including on tasks with "emergent" reputations; finding a capability a larger model has and a loss-matched smaller model lacks would be genuine evidence for size-dependent emergence.* Du et al. (2024) established it for their own models on standard benchmarks. `experiments.md` filed Experiment 5 as "not runnable here — lab-scale, a challenge to whoever has the cluster," because the literal design trains models of different sizes to a common loss.

Nobody has to train them. EleutherAI trained eight Pythia sizes on the same data in the same order with the same tokenizer and published 154 checkpoints of each. A larger Pythia model passes through every loss level a smaller one ever reaches, earlier in its training. So for any pair of sizes (s, L) with s < L there is a checkpoint of L at which L's loss on a fixed held-out slice of the Pile equals s's final loss, to within the checkpoint spacing — and the pair can be read on 2c's 34-task battery with the harness, floors and bar every experiment in this program has used. The question:

> Across the 21 size pairs of Pythia 160m–12b, when the larger model is read at the checkpoint where its Pile loss matches the smaller model's final loss, does its capability profile on the 34-task battery differ from the smaller model's final profile by more than the larger model's own profile differs between neighbouring checkpoints at that loss — and if so, in which direction?

Three answers matter to the essay, and §6 writes each one's licence in advance. **MATCHED:** the between-size discrepancy at equal loss is not distinguishable from the within-size wobble at that loss — Prediction 3 holds on this battery at this resolution, and the essay's paragraph moves from "confirmed by Du et al." to "held on a second family, a second battery, size ratios to 75×." **NOT-MATCHED · LARGE-AHEAD:** the larger model at equal loss is ahead of what the smaller ever becomes — the prediction's own named falsifier, size-dependent emergence measured at lab scale. **NOT-MATCHED · SMALL-AHEAD:** the annealed smaller model is ahead of the larger at equal loss — aggregate loss is not the whole of fidelity (exposure, annealing or the loss's composition carry the rest), which is also a departure from the prediction as written. §3.4's performability ledger prints the named cells for the falsifier sentence in every world.

## 2. What is known, what the design session computed, and what is sealed

**Known from the closed record, to everyone.** 2c's final-checkpoint argmax counts at 2.8b / 6.9b / 12b on all 34 rungs (the m4 records; the per-rung accuracies are printed in `experiment-2d-design.md` §4); 2d's final-checkpoint argmax counts at 410m and 1b (`experiments/exp2d/results/argmax/`); 2g's 21-point 2.8b trajectory and 2h's 22-point 6.9b trajectory at 10k-step spacing, per item; the eight 12b points of 2g's replication. So the **finals** of five of the seven sizes are known at count grain, and the **coarse shape** of two large-side trajectories is known. What no record holds: any Pythia loss on any held-out slice at any checkpoint; the finals of 160m and 1.4b; any read of 410m, 1b, 1.4b or 12b between step 0 and its final beyond 2g's eight 12b points; and any checkpoint of any size at 1000-step resolution.

**What the design session computed (2026-09-22, analysis-only on committed bytes, zero model contact; the four scripts are in this session's ledger and will be committed under `experiments/exp5/design_session/` at the build).** Each is a disclosure event:

1. **Rung-level clear-status flips between adjacent committed checkpoints** (2d's floor and bar applied to 2g's 2.8b and 2h's 6.9b grids): among adjacent pairs where at least one side clears, the status flips in **.224 of pairs at 2.8b (17 of 76) and .319 at 6.9b (30 of 94)** at 10k-step spacing; late in training (≥ 100k) 2 of 34 at 2.8b. Transient clears and checkpoint-local collapses are the texture 2g/2h reported; this is their rate.
2. **Count differences between adjacent committed checkpoints on the same live pairs:** mean |Δcount| **23.0 items at 2.8b (median 18, p90 40) and 16.7 at 6.9b (median 12, p90 41)**; late-training (≥ 60k) 21.6 and 13.3. At 1000-step spacing both should be smaller; nothing committed measures that, so §4 treats these as upper bounds.
3. **A power envelope for a performability-grain primary** (each cell's read a binary clear at 2d's bar; the between-size discordance minus the within-size discordance under a rung-block sign flip): with seven rungs carrying signal — the most the battery's rising set can supply — P(fire) ≤ .12 even when a third of live cells are truly discordant, because seven blocks give a p floor of 1/128 and the binary read discards the count. **This computation retired the performability read as the primary.** It survives as §3.4's ledger, where the essay's falsifier sentence is actually written.
4. **The same envelope at count grain** (§3.5's statistic): with a true between-size offset of 25 items (.05) on half the live cells, per-read noise of 6–10 items, and 9–11 rungs carrying cells, P(p < .01) is **.76–1.00**; over seven rungs .35–.62; at 15 items of noise .32–.52. The null's mean T is **negative** (−.005 to −.008) by construction (§3.5), which is the conservative direction. An effect bar of .02 defeats every one of these alternatives (mean T under them is .013–.017); a bar of .01 does not. §4 carries the table; the sealed power record supersedes it.

**What those four computations say, stated as the designer's belief and not as evidence:** the instrument has to read counts, not clears; the noise it has to beat is a between-checkpoint wobble of order 10–20 items per 500 at 10k spacing; and the detectable effect is an average excess discrepancy of about five items per 500 across live cells, with a blind region below that. No number in (1)–(4) is an outcome of this experiment. None of them touched a loss.

**What is sealed:** every loss (the whole matching axis); every read of every size between its first and final checkpoint at 1000-step resolution; the finals of 160m and 1.4b; hence every bracket, every window, every cell, T, the modifier and every secondary. **What is not sealed and the projection may use, disclosed:** the five known finals; 2g's and 2h's coarse trajectories, which bound what the 2.8b and 6.9b windows can show at 10k grain. The projection is graded on the window reads and on the 160m/410m/1b/1.4b/12b trajectories, which nothing committed constrains.

**Disclosure rule (checklist item 27)** applies from the build's first commit: every execution of the analyzer against the real tree before the tag is counted and printed in §2 at the tag.

## 3. Instrument

### 3.1 Models, checkpoints and the loss slice

**Sizes and pairs.** Pythia **160m, 410m, 1b, 1.4b, 2.8b, 6.9b, 12b** (`EleutherAI/pythia-{size}`, the non-deduplicated line 2c used), seven sizes, **21 ordered pairs (s, L), s < L**, size ratios 1.2× to 75×. 70m is out by default (dial b): its final clears nothing on any committed evidence and every larger model reaches its loss inside the log head, where the checkpoint spacing doubles per step. Each pair's small side is read at its **final** (`main`, 2c's pinned sha for 410m/1b/2.8b/6.9b/12b; 160m and 1.4b pinned at the build from the Hub scan of 2026-09-22: `50f5173d…`, `fedc38a1…`). Each pair's large side is read at a **window** of six trained checkpoints around the loss crossing (§3.2).

**The checkpoint list.** Every size carries 154 revisions `step0`, `step1`, `step2`, … `step512` (the log head), `step1000` … `step143000` (every 1000) — verified for all eight sizes on 2026-09-22 (155 branches each, `step143000` present). 2g's finding A stands for every size: a revision may carry `main`'s weight file as a stale copy beside its own, and a naive `from_pretrained(revision=…)` prefers it. **2g's candidate-file rule is applied per revision at the build** (shards if present; else the single safetensors if its LFS sha differs from `main`'s; else the `.bin`), the inventory refreshed and committed per size, every candidate's LFS sha pinned, a revision whose candidate sha tuple equals another's **excluded and disclosed** (2.8b's `step64000` is one such, 2g finding B), and the runner hashes what it downloaded and the loader digests the tensors. **The available list** per size = the trained revisions (step ≥ 1) that survive exclusion, in step order; "adjacent" below means adjacent on that list. Step 0 is never in a window.

**The loss slice.** `monology/pile-uncopyrighted`, revision `3be90335…`, file `val.jsonl.zst` (338,045,152 bytes, **LFS sha256 `db5e5d1532bf8dc33a6589b50ecba1a8c96f7b4b9cb343d168e603c393007c26`**) — the Pile's held-out validation split with the copyrighted subsets removed, each line carrying `meta.pile_set_name`. The slice: documents **in file order**, each tokenized with 2c's pinned Pythia tokenizer (no BOS; Pythia prepends nothing), **truncated at 2048 tokens**, documents under 64 tokens skipped and counted, accumulated until the scored-token total first reaches **2^21 = 2,097,152** (dial c). The token-id tensor is written once at the build, committed (`experiments/exp5/slice_5.npz`, ≈ 5 MB) with its sha pinned in the battery module, and re-derived from the file by a cold tool. **Per-token loss** = −log p(x_t | x_<t) in nats from an **fp32 log-softmax of the fp16 logits**, the first token of each document unscored; the **slice loss** ℓ = the token-weighted mean; per-set losses ℓ_k the same within each `pile_set_name`. Batch composition is pinned (documents in slice order, right-padded, batch size fixed at the build) so the value is a deterministic function of the weights on a host; the on-host determinism gate (§3.7) is the check.

**Why this slice and not the training loss.** Du et al. matched on training loss; Pythia's is not published per checkpoint and cannot be recovered without the training batches. A fixed held-out slice makes the matching axis a measured quantity with a pinned input, the same for every model, and every model's value on it is paired with every other's on the same tokens. **Disclosed:** ℓ is the loss on THIS slice; two models matched on it are matched on ≈ 2M validation tokens, not on the Pile. §5's S3 prints how the per-set components compare at the match.

**Loading.** Weights in fp16 (2g/2h's dtype; the identity digest measured from the loaded tensors); generation and the loss forward in fp16 with the loss in fp32 as above. The preflight (§7) checks finiteness at 12b `step1000` and `step1`, the earliest checkpoints any window can reach.

### 3.2 The matching rule and the window

For each small size s, the **target** ℓ_s = s's slice loss at its final. For each L > s:

1. **The spine.** L's slice loss at `step1000, 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000, 32000, 64000, 100000` and its final (nine points; `64000` replaced by the nearest available step where excluded). Every spine point is a full unit (§3.3): loss AND the 34-rung battery.
2. **The crossing interval.** The FIRST spine interval [t_i, t_{i+1}] with ℓ_L(t_i) ≥ ℓ_s > ℓ_L(t_{i+1}). A later re-crossing is texture, printed, never used. If no interval crosses (ℓ_L(final) ≥ ℓ_s — the larger model never reaches the smaller's loss), the pair is **dropped and printed**.
3. **Refinement.** Bisection over the available list within the interval — load the available step nearest the midpoint, score it, halve — until the crossing sits between two ADJACENT available steps **t_lo < t_hi** with ℓ_L(t_lo) ≥ ℓ_s > ℓ_L(t_hi). That pair is the **bracket A**.
4. **The window.** A plus the two available steps before t_lo (**B⁻**) and the two after t_hi (**B⁺**): six trained checkpoints, every one a full unit. At the list's edges (t_hi = the final, or t_lo within two of `step1`) the missing side is absent and printed; a cell with neither side is excluded from the primary (§3.4).

Every loaded checkpoint is scored on both instruments regardless of whether it ends up in a window; the search decides only which checkpoints exist in the record, and the analyzer re-derives every bracket and window from the committed loss table (gate 4). The **loss residuals** ℓ_L(t_lo) − ℓ_s ≥ 0 and ℓ_s − ℓ_L(t_hi) > 0 are printed per pair, with the bracket's width in steps and in tokens (2,097,152 tokens per step).

**Disclosed asymmetries the matching does not remove.** At its bracket the larger model has seen fewer tokens than the smaller model's 300 B (t_lo × 2.1 M), is under a higher learning rate (Pythia's cosine schedule decays to 10 % at the final), and is not annealed. Equal loss, unequal exposure and unequal optimizer state: a SMALL-AHEAD reading has these as its first candidates (§6), and S6 prints the exposure ratio per pair.

### 3.3 The outcome reads

A **unit** = (size, step): the checkpoint loaded under the candidate-file rule, the slice loss and its per-set components measured, then **2c's argmax harness verbatim** (`render_prompt` / `normalize_answer` / `verify`, 2-shot, greedy, `MAX_NEW_TOKENS` per answer type, batch 16) over the 34 rungs' 500 eval items each — per-item bits and continuations stored as 2h's records store them, counts of 500 per rung — then the checkpoint freed. **Floors** are 2d's frozen per-rung floors (majority share, or max(majority, 1/n_options) on the six option-listing rungs; `experiments/exp2d/battery_2d.floor_table`), the **bar** 2d's one-sided exact binomial at α .01 with rate > floor (`stats_2d.binomial_bar`) — the same bar 2g/2h/2i/2l/2m/2n/4/4c read their trajectories with. The 34 item files, the 2-shot exemplars and the criterion pins (`CRITERION_TRUNCATED_PIN` for base12_digitsum/base13) are 2d's, sha-checked at every load.

### 3.4 The cell statistic and the performability ledger

A **cell** is (pair, rung): 21 × 34 = 714. Its reads, in counts of 500: **f** = the small final; **a** = the mean of the two bracket counts (t_lo, t_hi); **b⁻** = the mean of the two B⁻ counts; **b⁺** = the mean of the two B⁺ counts.

**Live:** at least one of the seven reads clears 2d's bar. Cells where nothing clears anywhere carry no information about a capability and are **excluded and counted** (they are most of the 714: 23 of the 34 rungs never clear at any committed final).

**The cell statistic.** The **between-size discrepancy** R = |a − f| and the **within-size discrepancy** P = ½(|a − b⁻| + |a − b⁺|) (the available side alone at an edge; a cell with neither side is excluded and counted). The cell's contribution **c = (R − P) / 500**, in units of accuracy. Under Prediction 3 the smaller model's final is one more read of "the profile at loss ℓ_s," so R is a discrepancy between two reads at the same loss and P is a discrepancy between two reads whose loss displacement is LARGER (B sits 1–3 available steps beyond the bracket, on either side, while ℓ_s sits inside it): E[c] ≤ 0. Under size-dependence a true offset enters R and not P: E[c] > 0. The construction is conservative toward MATCHED by design, and §2 (4) measured the cost.

**The performability ledger** (S1, printed in every world, no α): per live cell, σ = [f clears], λ_lo/λ_hi = [the two bracket counts clear], and the class — **L-AHEAD** (λ_lo = λ_hi = 1, σ = 0: performable at a loss no lower than the smaller's final, stably, where the smaller never is), **S-AHEAD** (λ_lo = λ_hi = 0, σ = 1), **CONCORDANT** (all three 1), **UNSTABLE** (λ_lo ≠ λ_hi), with the loss residuals beside each L-AHEAD and S-AHEAD cell and, for L-AHEAD, whether B⁻ also clears (performable at a strictly higher loss). This ledger is where the essay's falsifier sentence is written: it names cells; it does not carry a p (§2 item 3 is why).

### 3.5 The primary statistic and its null

**T = mean of c over live cells with a defined P.** Bar: **T ≥ .01** (an excess between-size discrepancy of five items per 500, averaged over live cells; dial e). **Null:** the exact **rung-block sign flip** of the per-rung sums of c — under exchangeability of "the small final" with "a neighbouring bracket of the large model at the same loss," each rung's block sum is symmetric about zero; enumerated over the rungs with a nonzero block sum when they number ≤ 20, sampled 10,000 times otherwise; **p = P(T_null ≥ T)**, one-sided. Rung is the block because a rung's cells share the rung (its items, its floor, its transient habits — odd6 flips seven times on 6.9b's grid); the family-block flip (16 families, 65,536 signings) and the cell-level flip are printed beside it as sensitivities, never governing. The null's mean is below zero on the real dependence (§2 item 4): a rejection is a rejection against a conservative reference, and a MATCHED reading is read against the resolution §4 states.

### 3.6 The direction modifier

Among live cells with R > P (the cells whose between-size discrepancy exceeds their own within-size one), the sign of a − f: **LARGE-AHEAD** if an exact two-sided binomial at .05 rejects with more positive signs; **SMALL-AHEAD** with more negative; **MIXED** otherwise; **THIN** when fewer than 8 such cells exist (no test). The signed mean offset (a − f)/500 over all live cells is printed with a rung-clustered bootstrap CI. The modifier is read only in NOT-MATCHED; in MATCHED it is printed as texture.

### 3.7 Gates (INSUFFICIENT_DATA on failure)

0. **Host attestation.** The stack pins (torch 2.12.1, transformers 5.13.0, numpy 2.4.6, safetensors, tokenizers, huggingface_hub — the Mac's, on the host's Python 3.11), the device, the GPU, both download transports measured and the one used recorded (4c note 2); every unit record carries them; a record from a different stack or device is refused at load.
1. **Identity and known answers.** (a) The 2.8b final through **two loader paths** on the host — 2c's `main` loader and the candidate-file `step143000` loader — with equal tensor digests, identical continuations on all 34 rungs and an identical slice loss to the bit. (b) The seven finals' counts against the Mac's committed counts where a referent exists (2c's m4 at 2.8b/6.9b/12b; 2d's at 410m/1b; 160m and 1.4b have none — disclosed): per rung |Δ| ≤ 15 and Σ|Δ| ≤ 120 over the 34 rungs. The tolerances are pinned at the build from the one measurement that exists — the 2026-09-20 A100 benchmark's 6.9b final against the Mac's: max |Δ| 8, Σ|Δ| 57, near-tie flips in fp16 — and sit an order of magnitude below what a different network produces (hundreds on antonym alone). (c) The spine units that coincide with 2g's/2h's committed grids (2.8b: 1k, 2k, 4k, 8k, 16k, 32k, 100k; 6.9b: those and 64k) under the same tolerance — fifteen more known-answer checkpoints, interior to training, read before any window. On the Mac (dial a's alternative) (b) and (c) are byte-identity gates.
2. **The slice.** The committed token tensor re-derived from the pinned file (sha, length, document count, skipped count) and equal.
3. **Units.** Every unit: candidate LFS shas equal to the manifest's, tensor digest recorded, fp16 load, finite loss, 500/500 coverage on all 34 rungs, continuations present; a unit scored twice (a bisection revisit) must be byte-identical to its first record.
4. **Brackets.** For every kept pair the bracket re-derived from the committed loss table is adjacent on the available list and straddles ℓ_s; the window's members exist as units; the search log names every loaded step and nothing else was loaded.
5. **The power record** attached and equal to the sealed one; **the projection** committed before the first window unit.
6. **The import surface** pinned at entry and exit (lesson 11); every read the analyzer makes pinned or content-checked (read sweep 0 unpinned).
7. **Tags.** `exp5-preregistered` blob-bound over the analyzer, the battery/loss module, the search and sweep runners; `exp5-targets-sealed` over the finals' records, the loss table's first rows and the power record; the analyzer refuses without both and refuses a post-tag delta in any bound blob.

Every refusal is collected and delivered as INSUFFICIENT_DATA with its reason verbatim (lesson 8); every tree the runner can leave — halted mid-search, halted mid-window, a pair without a bracket — reaches that terminal from the production path, demonstrated at the freeze.

### 3.8 The tree

INSUFFICIENT_DATA (any gate) → **UNDETERMINED** (fewer than 20 live cells with a defined P, or fewer than 5 rungs carrying them — the resolution is not there) → **NOT-MATCHED** (p < .01 and T ≥ .01), with the modifier LARGE-AHEAD / SMALL-AHEAD / MIXED / THIN → **MATCHED** (otherwise), read under the power declaration with the blind region stated as the minimum detectable T.

### 3.9 Pins and storage

Per unit: the loss record (ℓ, per-set ℓ_k with token counts, the batch pins, dtype facts, digest) and 34 rung records in 2h's shape (per-item bits, continuations, count, sha of items). ≈ 230 units × ≈ 1 MB ≈ 230 MB committed — 4c's order (150–200 MB). The loss table (`loss_table_5.json`: size, step, ℓ, per-set) and the search log are committed as they grow, watcher-committed per unit. The activation-free design means no gitignored inputs on the host beyond the slice and the item files.

## 4. Cells, resolution and power — declared before any model contact

**Cells.** 714 nominal; live cells are where a capability shows on either side. From the known finals alone: at 2.8b's and 6.9b's finals 7–10 rungs clear; at 1b's and 410m's, one to three; 160m's and 1.4b's are unknown. A bracket read at a large model's early checkpoint can add live cells the finals do not predict (a rung that clears transiently mid-training does so on 2h's grid). The designer's expectation: **40–90 live cells over 8–11 rungs**, concentrated in the pairs whose small side is 1b or larger — the 160m and 410m rows contribute mostly through the large side.

**Noise.** §2 (2): mean |Δcount| of 17 items between adjacent committed checkpoints late in training at 10k spacing, on live rungs; the window's brackets are 1000 steps apart, so the per-read wobble is at most this and likely half.

**Design-stage envelope (§2 item 4; a model, superseded by the sealed record):** P(p < .01) under a 25-item offset on half the live cells —

| rungs × cells | noise 6 | noise 10 | noise 15 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 × 6 (66 live) | 1.00 | .93 | .52 |
| 9 × 5 (45) | .93 | .76 | .32 |
| 7 × 4 (28) | .62 | .35 | .10 |

with the null's mean T at −.005 to −.008 and its SD .001–.002 (the test is far from the bar under H0 in every profile). The same offset on a quarter of the live cells is inside the blind region at every noise level (P ≤ .36). **So the design detects a between-size excess of about five items per 500 averaged over live cells, and does not detect one of two or three.**

**The sealed power record, written ONCE at the targets-sealed stage** (after the seven finals and the loss targets exist, before any window): through the verdict's own code (§3.5), the live cells' small-side statuses real, the large-side statuses and the per-read noise drawn from a model whose parameters are the committed 10k-spacing differences (§2 item 2) scaled by a factor of 1 and ½ (both printed), the alternative a 25-item offset on half the live cells in one direction (D = .05, q = ½); P(NOT-MATCHED | D, q), the null SD of T, the minimum detectable T at .75, and the realized α of the whole rule — printed and **declared POWERED or UNDERPOWERED against the bar .75** before the projection is sealed (dial a: run in either case, declaration printed). Lesson 6: the alternative's SHAPE is declared — an offset concentrated on the option rungs (where the finals differ most across sizes) and one spread evenly are both simulated, both printed.

## 6. Licences, written in advance

- **MATCHED** (under POWERED): the essay's Prediction 3 paragraph gains a sentence — *on Pythia, across 21 size pairs to 75×, a larger model read at the checkpoint where its held-out loss equals a smaller model's final loss has the smaller model's profile on this battery to within the larger model's own wobble between neighbouring checkpoints* — and the scoreboard's line says the same at the resolution §4 states (an average excess of five items per 500 detectable, two or three not). Under UNDERPOWERED the sentence is "not distinguishable at this resolution" and nothing more. S1's ledger is quoted only for the named cells it contains.
- **NOT-MATCHED · LARGE-AHEAD:** the prediction's own falsifier at lab scale. The essay says so in the prediction's paragraph and the scoreboard: *at equal loss the larger Pythia models are ahead of what the smaller ones ever become on these tasks; loss is not sufficient for the profile on this battery.* The lens framing's "bits bought with loss" loses its cleanest quantitative form; the essay keeps the framing and loses the claim that loss is the whole currency. S3's composition reading is printed beside it because a matched aggregate over a different mix is the first alternative a reader will raise.
- **NOT-MATCHED · SMALL-AHEAD:** the annealed smaller model is ahead at equal loss. The prediction as written fails in the other direction; the licensed sentence names the candidates the design cannot separate — exposure, annealing, optimizer state, loss composition (S3, S6) — and the essay's paragraph says that aggregate loss under-describes fidelity, without choosing among them. Not licensed: "size-dependent emergence."
- **NOT-MATCHED · MIXED / THIN:** the discrepancy is real and has no direction the design can read; the essay reports the excess and the ledger, no mechanism sentence.
- **UNDETERMINED:** the battery did not supply the cells; the essay's paragraph is unchanged and `experiments.md` records the resolution reached.
- In every world the essay's paragraph keeps the caveat: one family, one battery of 34 synthetic tasks, sizes to 12b, loss matched on a 2M-token validation slice.

## 7. Run plan, host and model contact

**Host: a rented A100 80 GB on-demand (dial a; Michael's ruling of 2026-09-20 funded this experiment).** The Mac is the alternative and the host of S9. Reasons: the 6.9b argmax unit runs 12× faster on the A100 (276 s vs 3,321 s, benchmark of 2026-09-20); 12b enters at ≈ 8 min per unit against ≈ 80 on the Mac; and the campaign streams ≈ 2.7 TB, which the xet transport moves at 250–350 MB/s on a rented box against the Mac's tens. ## 8. Alternatives considered

- **Full loss-axis trajectories for every size** (Du et al.'s construction, count against loss for all sizes at a common grid, then "loss at first clear" per rung across sizes). Subsumes the pairwise design but needs each size at ≈ 20 points plus the loss at every committed 2.8b/6.9b/12b point — a re-download of ≈ 630 GB for losses alone — and reads emergence as "first clear," which §2 (1) shows is transient-prone. S2 gives the overlay from what the pairwise design loads anyway.
- **Matching at a mid-training loss of the small model as well as its final.** Symmetrizes the exposure and optimizer-state asymmetry (both sides mid-training) and doubles the cells; doubles the cost. Named as the successor if SMALL-AHEAD obtains.
- **Performability-grain primary.** Retired by §2 (3): seven rungs cannot carry a p below .01 under a rung-block flip, and the binary read discards the count. Kept as S1.
- **A single bracket (four checkpoints per pair, one placebo side).** Saves ≈ 40 loads (≈ 500 GB); the placebo then carries a systematic loss displacement in one direction. The symmetric window is recommended (dial d).
- **The Mac.** Free, byte-identity gates against every committed table, four to nine days, no 12b in practice. Dial a.
- **The deduplicated Pythia line.** Different weights, no committed referents; no.

## 9. What Experiment 5 does not claim

One family (Pythia), one battery (34 synthetic tasks, 11 of which any size ever clears at its final), sizes to 12b, loss matched on ≈ 2M held-out Pile tokens with per-checkpoint spacing of 1000 steps. A MATCHED reading is bounded by §4's resolution; it says nothing about capabilities this battery does not contain, about frontier sizes, or about the composition of the loss (S3 is descriptive). A NOT-MATCHED reading does not identify a mechanism: the design separates "at equal loss the profiles differ beyond the within-size wobble" from "they do not," and the modifier gives a direction; S3 and S6 give the named alternatives their first look and no more. The comparison is at the greedy channel only — no probe, no sampled rate; that ladder is a different experiment. Nothing here uses a model above 12b, and the small side is read only at its final.
