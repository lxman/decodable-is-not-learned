================================================================
SCOPE -- WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY
================================================================
1. ONE STRATUM CARRIED THE TEST.  len-5 and len-6 produced zero fires
   across 470,016 new draws.  Section 8's "within-length by
   construction" defence holds, but only len-4 carried information.
2. C1 COLLAPSED TO "HAS A REPEATED CHARACTER."  The binary len-4 tie
   structure was printed at freeze as exactly this risk and it landed.
   C1's finer gradations were never exercised; this is not a test of
   unigram entropy as a graded predictor.
3. NO MECHANISM CLAIM (section 9).  A live alternative this experiment
   cannot exclude: low-entropy answers are a priori more probable
   strings, and answers sharing more characters with their own input
   are more reachable by copy-like emission -- and ctrl_copy shows
   copying is near-perfect.  "Compressibility forecasts fires" is a
   statement about a frozen functional and a frozen event, not about
   circuits or partial reversal competence.
4. NO CROSS-FAMILY GENERALIZATION.  reverse_string only, Pythia
   410m/1b, T = 1.0 untruncated, these budgets.  rev_string7's standing
   bounds are untouched; no closed experiment is re-adjudicated.
5. THE POWER MODEL WAS MIS-SHAPED, NOT MERELY PESSIMISTIC.  Declared
   underpowered in advance at .2616; rejected at 1.6e-04.  The frozen
   alternative modelled ITEM-level concentration on the 13 committed
   fires with add-lambda smoothing; the realized structure is a
   CLASS-level contrast over 45 items -- a far larger target.  The
   miss was in the alternative's shape, not its size.
