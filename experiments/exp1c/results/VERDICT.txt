THE HYPOTHESIS. Below the percolation threshold, does linearly decodable class
structure accumulate with graph density while the capability metric stays flat?
Measured across 0.25 / 0.45 / 0.65 / 0.85 p_c on 40 cells that exp1's S3 graph
branch trained during the 1b campaign and from which one scalar each had been
taken — 1,800 checkpoints the probe had never touched.

ANSWER, within the declared resolution: no. Nothing is decodable below p_c —
not at depth, not at layer 0, not above the twin at any of the 8 sites in any
of the 40 cells.

sd .0566 against a mean of .0246 — a spread more than twice the effect.
