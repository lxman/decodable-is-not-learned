DEFECT FOUND IN THE OUTCOME OPERATIONALIZATION — found before the verdict
was computed, and recorded in that order. Design §3 normalizes the ascent
margin against EMPIRICAL UNTRAINED FLOORS. Those floors are ~0 for every
rung, because an untrained Pythia emits malformed text rather than wrong
answers. A trained model that has acquired only the OUTPUT FORMAT — "emit
a small integer" — therefore scores at 1/|answer space| and is credited
that entire rate as capability. Ten rungs sat at their guessing rate;
odd_one_out scored .208 against its 1/4 = .250 chance — BELOW chance — and
was credited .207 ascent, ranking fifth. Only antonym (.560 vs .250) and
antonym6 (.398 vs .167) clear chance decisively.

This is the methods paper's own thesis one level up: ABOVE THE UNTRAINED
FLOOR IS NOT THE SAME AS CAPABLE, exactly as decodable is not learned. It
is a defect in the operationalization, not a coding error.

The frozen rule was NOT changed. As a disclosed descriptive, never
verdict-touching: chance-corrected rho = .2005 (block p .3989), 13 nonzero
rungs against the frozen 25. Correcting the artifact makes the correlation
WEAKER — the contamination was manufacturing part of the little signal
present, not masking a real one.

SCIENTIFIC CONTENT — two findings that outlive the omnibus test.

  1. DECODABLE BUT NOT GENERABLE, measured. The reversal family carries
     the 2nd and 3rd highest probe margins in the battery (rev_string7
     .699, reverse_string .624) and scores EXACTLY 0.000 argmax ascent at
     all three eval sizes. Character-level reversal is highly decodable
     because the information sits in the token sequence, and reliably
     ungenerable at this scale. This was predicted explicitly in the
     preregistered projection. It is the essay's own "necessary, not
     sufficient" limitation converted from caveat to measurement.

  2. THE NAMED DISCONFIRMER FIRED. sub3_mid (probe .000 -> ascent .190)
     and arith_next (probe .000 -> .175) are probe-flat with real eval
     signal that SURVIVES the chance correction. The projection named "a
     probe-flat rung with substantial ascent" as the sharpest available
     disconfirmation: structure absent below threshold that nonetheless
     appears above it is the Lubana-style percolation case the essay
     carves out, not the resolution case it argues for. sub3_mid was
     investigated for a scoring artifact and CLEARED by reading raw
     generations — 2.8b genuinely outperforms 6.9b and 12b on 3-digit
     subtraction (near-miss errors against wild ones), a real
     non-monotonicity.

  3. LAYER-0 RESERVOIR CHANNEL, characterized and contained. Every
     cross-size identical accuracy in the probe records sits at
     best_layer 0, and 72 of 72 trained-vs-untrained layer-0 pairs are
     identical: a layer-0 fit reads the embedding and is fixed by the
     items and the split, independent of weights and of width. That is
     Exp 2's reservoir channel alive inside 2c's records. It is harmless
     here for exactly one reason, asserted by test: NO PRESENT m3 FIT
     SITS AT LAYER 0.

METHODOLOGICAL NOTE FOR ANY SUCCESSOR. A scale-ascent score defined as a
MEAN across sizes reads a 2.8b spike that collapses at 6.9b/12b as
"moderate ascent" (sub3_mid: .528 / .028 / .022 -> .193). Monotonicity is
not tested by the mean. Any successor should either require monotone
ascent or report the profile alongside the scalar.
