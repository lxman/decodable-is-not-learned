EXPERIMENT 3a — FORMAL VERDICT (recorded 2026-08-15)

VERDICT: INSUFFICIENT_DATA

    cause     the frozen analysis cannot execute on the battery it was
              frozen against
    campaign  24 of 24 cells collected, 0 failures, 81.2 min
    gate      replication: 9 of 12 trained cells reproduce 2c's committed
              accuracy EXACTLY; 3 have no referent to reproduce

Mechanism. The frozen verdict tree (design §5) adjudicates a replication
check over every trained cell:

    abs(float(c["full_string_acc"]) - float(c["committed_2c_acc"])) > 0.02

`ctrl_copy` was never evaluated by Exp 2c on the eval ladder. It exists in
2c as a probe-side known_present control and as gate 4 at the PROBE sizes
(410m/1b, .960/.980); there is no m4 record at 2.8b/6.9b/12b. So
`committed_2c_acc` is None for three trained cells and `float(None)` raises
TypeError. The analysis does not return a wrong verdict — it cannot run.

WHAT THE COLLECTED DATA DOES SHOW, and it is only the gate:
    clock24_d999   2.8b .0440  6.9b .0580  12b .0500   all == 2c committed
    rev_string7    2.8b .0000  6.9b .0000  12b .0000   all == 2c committed
    reverse_string 2.8b .0000  6.9b .0020  12b .0000   all == 2c committed
    ctrl_copy      2.8b .9900  6.9b .9960  12b .9940   no 2c referent

The reverse_string/6.9b value of .0020 independently reproduces the single
correct item in five hundred that this program corrected in the methods
paper earlier the same day.

THE PRIMARY STATISTIC WAS NEVER COMPUTED. First-character accuracy has not
been calculated for any cell, at any size, in any mode. The verdict tree
halts at the replication gate, which precedes the claim, and no analysis was
run past it. This is stated because it is the condition under which a
successor may be designed: nobody has seen the outcome.

WHY THIS IS INSUFFICIENT_DATA AND NOT AN AMENDMENT. Three amendments were
available and all were declined by Michael in favour of the strict reading:
skipping referentless cells as a defect fix, spending the one pre-committed
change on the same edit, or ruling ctrl_copy out of the gate. Each would have
been made while holding the data, and each would have made INSUFFICIENT_DATA
marginally less reachable. The instrument was frozen against a battery it
could not adjudicate; that is the instrument's result, and the program's
rule is that a frozen criterion binds.

THE DEFECT WAS DISCOVERABLE AT FREEZE TIME. Open item 4 of the design
confirmed the untrained twin was constructible. Nothing confirmed that every
trained cell had a committed 2c value for the gate to compare against. The
freeze checklist verified that every input verdict() takes has a frozen
PRODUCER — 1c's gap — and did not verify that every input has a defined
VALUE on this battery.

THE ONE PRE-COMMITTED CHANGE WAS NOT SPENT.

THE COLLECTED CELLS ARE KEPT. Greedy decoding over fixed prompts is
deterministic, so a successor re-running the same cells must reproduce these
continuations byte for byte. They are retained as the reproduction referent
for that successor, not as its data.
