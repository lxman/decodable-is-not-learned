## 1. The question

Experiment 5 closed NOT-MATCHED · MIXED (tag `exp5-closed` at e0f63e90e): at equal held-out loss, a larger Pythia model's profile on the 34-task battery differs from the smaller model's final profile by more than the larger model's own wobble between neighbouring checkpoints — T .0535, i.e. an excess of **27 items per 500** averaged over 63 live cells, against a bar of five, rung-block p .00061 — with no direction (24 cells each way). The essay carries it in three places (the empirical paragraph, Prediction 3, the scoreboard) with three disclosures beside it, and the third disclosure is the one this experiment is about: **the smaller model's final was one un-averaged read.** The cell statistic c = (R − P)/500 subtracts the LARGE side's within-size wobble P (the bracket mean against the two neighbouring checkpoint pairs) from the between-size discrepancy R = |a − f|, and takes the small final f at face value. Experiment 5's power record modelled f as fixed (§4 of its design: "f itself is fixed (a committed read)"). Its S11 then showed that the last 1,000 steps of Pythia's anneal move a rising rung by up to 75 items (2.8b sub3_mid 189 → 264; arith_next +23; sub_base8 −21; 6.9b median7 −26, count_div13 −24; 1.4b hamming12 +32) — so R contains a small-side read noise that P does not, and Experiment 5's rule is calibrated only while that noise is small. Retrospective note 7: *noise on the small side too — a successor reads the small final's own neighbours.* This is that successor.

> With the smaller model read at its final AND at its three preceding checkpoints (142000, 141000, 140000), so that the small side's own moves to its neighbours are measured the way the large side's were, does Experiment 5's excess survive subtracting BOTH sides' wobble — T_sym = mean over the same 63 cells of (R − M)/500 ≥ .01 at rung-block p < .01, with M the matched within-size discrepancy of §3.2 (each side's own move to a neighbouring checkpoint, combined at the weight that gives M the noise law of R) — or was the excess, in whole or in part, the small final's read noise?

**The stake is the essay's Prediction 3 sentence.** Under SURVIVES the disclosure is retired and the excess is stated net of both wobbles. Under NOT-SURVIVED · INSIDE the excess is demoted to "not distinguishable from the two sides' combined wobble at this resolution" and "the answer is partly no" retreats to "not distinguishable at this resolution" — Prediction 3 back toward MATCHED on this battery (§6). The intermediate cells (§3.7) are named in advance because the designer expects one of them to be live: the 2.8b row's arithmetic cells are so large (sub3_mid: f 264 against bracket reads of 0–9) that no wobble of the measured order absorbs them, while the other rows sit at 10–18 items per 500, inside the range a symmetric subtraction can move.

Nothing on the large side is re-read, no bracket is re-matched, and Experiment 5's verdict is not revised in any world; what changes is the sentence it licenses (4b's pattern).

None of this is evidence.

**What Experiment 5's calibration depends on, stated analytically (no data).** Write σ_L for the large side's per-read SD on a count and σ_s for the small side's; a is a two-read mean, f one read; under pure read noise with no between-size structure, a − f has variance σ_L²/2 + σ_s², while each of P's two terms, a − b_side, has variance σ_L² (two two-read means). Experiment 5's c = R − P therefore has mean zero only at σ_s = σ_L/√2 ≈ 10.7 items (at its σ_L of 15.07); below that it over-subtracts — its power record's negative null mean of −.005 to −.008 at σ_s = 0 is this √2 excess — and above it the rule fires on nothing. **The obvious symmetric extension, R − ½(P + Q) with Q = mean_j |f − n_j| (each term of variance 2σ_s²), is calibrated only near σ_L ≈ √2·σ_s:** E|X| is proportional to X's SD, so its null mean is .798·[√(σ_L²/2 + σ_s²) − ½(√(σ_L² + 16) + √(2σ_s² + 16))] items (16 the shared drift's variance), which is +0.7 items at σ_s = 0, within ±0.5 between 5 and 20, +2.2 at 30 and **+5.2 items — the bar — at 45**: anti-conservative exactly where the small side is noisiest, the regime this experiment exists to test. So the primary uses a **matched placebo** instead (§3.2): for each large-side outer window s and small-side member j, D_sj = |(a − b_s) + (f − n_j)| / √2 — the large side's own move plus the small side's own move, scaled so that under the null D_sj has variance σ_L²/2 + σ_s², R's law, at EVERY (σ_L, σ_s); the loss displacements that enter b_s and n_j but not a − f make it conservative. With a noiseless small side (n_j = f) it reduces to R − P/√2, the correctly scaled version of Experiment 5's statistic. The sealed power record prints the realized null mean of all three forms on a σ_s grid (§4).

**What is sealed:** the 24 reads this experiment makes — for each of the six small sides, its final and `step142000` re-read on the 5b box (gate 1, and M's and Q's own-box reads), and `step141000` and `step140000` (new); hence every Q, T_sym, σ̂_s, the sub-cell, every secondary that touches a 5b read. **What is not sealed and the projection may use, disclosed:** all of Experiment 5's record including the six committed S11 units. ### 3.2 The cell statistic

For each of Experiment 5's **63 live cells with a defined P** — taken verbatim from Experiment 5's committed cell table, re-derived through Experiment 5's frozen code (gate 2); liveness is NOT re-evaluated and the small-side members add no cells (dial f) — with a, b⁻, b⁺, R = |a − f_A| and P the committed box-A values:

- **f_B** = the small final's count on box B; **n_j** = the count at member j ∈ W_s on box B (dial c: Q and M use box B's small-side reads only; R keeps Experiment 5's f_A).
- **The matched placebo M** = the mean over the six combinations (s ∈ {B⁻, B⁺}, j ∈ W_s) of **D_sj = |(a − b_s) + (f_B − n_j)| / √2** — each side's own move to a neighbour, added, at the weight that gives D_sj the noise variance of a − f under the null (§2; dial d). The sign is "added" (dial e): the placebo's correlation with a − f is then (σ_L²/2 − σ_s²)/(σ_L²/2 + σ_s²), near zero across the designer's prior; the subtracted form has the same mean and a positively correlated placebo.
- **Q = mean over j of |f_B − n_j|** — the small side's wobble in Experiment 5's units, printed beside P (S3), governing nothing.
- **c_sym = (R − M) / 500** (dial d). Under Prediction 3, E[c_sym] ≤ 0 (equal at pure noise, below it by the displacements); under a between-size offset at equal loss the offset enters R and cancels inside every D_sj (both of its pieces are within-side differences). The naive form (R − ½(P + Q))/500 and the conservative form (R − max(P, Q))/500 are printed as S10 with their null means from the grid.

A side with fewer than three members (a non-finite member is ABSENT under Experiment 5's slip-9 rule: its loss not finite, its counts never read, the side shorter, printed — except the re-read member 142000, whose non-finite loss fails gate 1's identity comparison with Experiment 5's finite committed read and refuses) takes M over the available (s, j) combinations; a cell whose large side has one outer window (an edge cell — Experiment 5 had none) likewise; a non-finite final halts the size (Experiment 5's writer halts on a non-finite final) and refuses; a side cannot lose all three members without losing 142000, which refuses at gate 1 — so no side is excluded on a finished campaign (freeze F-4).

### 3.3 The primary statistic and its null

**T_sym = mean of c_sym over the live cells with M defined.** Bar **T_sym ≥ .01** (five items per 500), **α .01**, null = Experiment 5's exact **rung-block sign flip** of the per-rung sums of c_sym (`stats_5.sign_flip_p_5` verbatim; enumerated over the rungs with a nonzero block sum when ≤ 20), family-block and cell-level flips printed beside it — every constant Experiment 5's (dial g), so that the only thing that changed between the two verdicts is what was subtracted. The sign-flip null's exchangeability is now "a between-size discrepancy at equal loss against the two sides' own moves to their neighbours, combined at R's noise law"; §2 gives the analytic argument and §4 the simulated null mean.

**Stated in items throughout:** T × 500. Experiment 5's excess is 27 items; the bar is five.

### 3.4 The calibration read (Exp 4 R-7 and 4b, applied in advance)

The sealed power record (§4) prints, on a grid of small-side noise σ_s ∈ {5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45} items, the **realized α of this experiment's whole rule** (p < .01 ∧ T_sym ≥ .01) under the null, and the null mean and SD of T_sym. The analyzer measures **σ̂_s** = the pooled per-read SD of the small side's four box-B reads over the live cells (the sample variance of {f_B, n₁₄₂₀₀₀, n₁₄₁₀₀₀, n₁₄₀₀₀₀} per cell, averaged, square-rooted; per side printed too — 4b note 1) and reads the realized α at σ̂_s by linear interpolation on the grid — on the axis of the SIMULATED σ̂_s (the same estimator applied to each grid point's simulated reads, which includes the modelled drift), not the nominal σ_s, so the lookup is on the quantity the analyzer measures; below the grid's bottom the bottom value is read (the rule is conservative there by §2). **The grid must bracket σ̂_s above** (Exp 4's lesson: its λ̂ fell above its grid's top and the licence was not claimed): σ̂_s above the simulated axis's top (the estimator's mean at σ_s = 45) is the OFF-GRID sub-cell; the simulated axis must be strictly increasing, else the power gate refuses (freeze F-3). **T\*** = T_sym − the null mean at σ̂_s is printed in items beside T_sym (4b's convention), descriptive.
